# Qofeno hero and Three.js research

X search returned HTTP 403 in the cloud browser. Public search index results led to X’s official profile embeds and oEmbed responses. The 30 hero posts below were reviewed through their images or video previews. The 30 Three.js posts were checked against their published text, and showcase pages and visual previews were used where available.

## What changed on Qofeno

- A clearer headline and shorter explanation describe the board before the artwork. On narrow screens the primary action appears above the Canvas illustration.
- The larger Canvas composition keeps its globe and layered paper. The lettering is readable from the first frame while the drawing and paper settle.
- The entire illustrated preview links to the live owner placement. The mint tag on the preview appears again on the 3D paper plaque, connecting the illustration to the real listing.
- The Three.js scene gives the plaque extra space on phones. One object, shared materials and on-demand drawing keep the interaction contained.
